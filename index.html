About this library

1 Pick a module and open it to see every quiz by week, with the topics each one covers.
2 Open the module's own directory or click a quiz to start.

Since starting Foundations, I've been making practice quiz sites weekly with the help of Gemini AI. The site design started out pretty basic since this began as a personal experiment, but I gradually upgraded it later on once I realized how useful these actually were for myself and others.

I’ve verified all the questions against our lecture PDFs, but I am just a boy after all. Despite my terrifying genius, a mistake might have slipped through here and there.

Keep in mind these align with the Class of 2029 lectures, so your curriculum may vary. If you have any questions, concerns, or just want to chat, feel free to reach out at ibecerra@augusta.edu.
